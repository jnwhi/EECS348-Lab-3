PHILOSOPHY

COMPUTATION AS MECHANICAL MASS

Over twelve years directing latency-critical clusters across continental networks, I view packet propagation not as ethereal bits, but as physical inertia. When a cluster reconciles petabytes across European regions, magnetic armatures oscillate, transformers hum at audible pitches, and metric tons of coolant circulate.

My kinetic installation practice materializes these latent computational realities into unvarnished raw brass, pneumatic relays, and counterweighted pendulums tuned directly to network telemetry.
